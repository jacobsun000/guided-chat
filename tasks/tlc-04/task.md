# Where Ride Availability Falls Short

## Content

Where and when does ride availability seem poorly matched to what travelers need?

## Deliverables

Submit a Markdown file named report.md identifying the places and times of concern, defining the supply and need proxies used, presenting supporting evidence, considering contradictory cases and alternative explanations, and distinguishing observations from recommendations.

`datasets/nyc_tlc_trip_records`
