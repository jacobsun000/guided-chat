# Comparable Applicants and Unequal Outcomes

## Content

Do similarly situated applicants receive meaningfully different mortgage outcomes?

## Deliverables

Submit a Markdown file named report.md documenting how comparable applicants and outcomes were defined, the adjustment or matching approach, group-level sample sizes and estimates with uncertainty, robustness checks, and a careful statement of what the analysis can and cannot establish.
