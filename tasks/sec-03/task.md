# Risks Companies Are Emphasizing

## Content

What risks and uncertainties are companies emphasizing in their disclosures?

## Deliverables

Submit a text file named answer.txt summarizing the risk themes you identified, naming representative companies or disclosures for each theme, explaining how themes were derived, noting meaningful variation or counterexamples, and describing coverage and interpretation limitations.
