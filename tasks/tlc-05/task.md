# How Well Specialized Ride Options Work

## Content

How well do shared and accessible ride options appear to work for New Yorkers?

## Deliverables

Submit a text file named answer.txt describing how you evaluated shared and accessible services, the metrics and comparisons used, the evidence supporting your assessment, important geographic or temporal differences, and limitations of the available service indicators.
